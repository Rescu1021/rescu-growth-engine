# v0.4 — Offer + Outreach Automation Layer

Adds target-specific partnership offers and personalized outreach copy.

## Offer design
Value-equation approach: lead with desired outcome, raise confidence with a clear physician-led pathway, reduce delay with a small pilot, and reduce effort by having Rescu handle navigation and scheduling. Each campaign gets a simple keyword CTA.

## Safety and trust
- No guarantees of medical outcomes.
- No fake scarcity.
- No patient-data targeting.
- Commercial email includes Rescu identity, physical address, and opt-out instruction.
- New outbound messages are REVIEW_REQUIRED by default.
- Suppression and opt-out must be checked before any sender integration transmits a message.

## Sending policy
1. A-tier, verified business contact only.
2. Personalized first message.
3. Maximum two follow-ups unless recipient engages.
4. Immediately suppress opt-outs, hard bounces and explicit disinterest.
5. Record replies, meetings and appointments as outcome signals.
