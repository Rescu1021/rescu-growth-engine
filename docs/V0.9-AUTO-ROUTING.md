# v0.9 — Automated Myrna / Ana Routing

## Purpose
Remove manual sorting while preserving human control of outbound communications.

## Routing
- Ana: aesthetics, TriLift, RF microneedling, facials/beauty collaborations.
- Myrna: HBOT athlete partnerships, gyms, clubs, fitness/performance, corporate and non-aesthetic partnership development.
- Dr. Taylor clinical review: physician/clinical referral relationships, including ozone-referral campaigns.
- Shared Review: ambiguous/cross-service opportunities.

## Automation
For every scored lead:
1. Determine campaign/service fit.
2. Assign owner.
3. Create owner-specific queue record.
4. Attach prepared target-specific copy/offer.
5. Set A-tier leads to REVIEW_AND_SEND.
6. Stop follow-up on reply, booking, opt-out, bad contact or hard bounce.
7. Feed outcomes/revenue back into scoring.

## Safety / quality gates
- No autonomous cold-email blast.
- No PHI in growth queues.
- No referral-linked credit for clinical referral sources by default.
- $100 Rescu Credit may be used only for eligible non-clinical promotional partners.
- Suppression list always wins.
- Ambiguous leads are reviewed rather than guessed.
