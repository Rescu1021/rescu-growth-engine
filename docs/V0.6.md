# v0.6 — Multi-Path Close + Partner Incentives

## Closing paths
Every target-specific offer can now close in two ways:
1. Reply with the campaign keyword.
2. Reply MEET or use the configured scheduling URL for a 15-minute Google Meet.

Set RESCU_MEETING_URL to the approved scheduling destination. The engine never invents a meeting URL.

## Incentives
Clinical campaigns default to no per-patient referral compensation. Non-clinical promotional/marketing partners can display an approved configurable bonus only after Rescu approves the amount and terms.

This separation is intentional. Healthcare referral compensation can implicate federal/state anti-kickback, fee-splitting, professional-conduct, payer and other rules depending on the parties and services. Legal review is required before activating any healthcare-referral compensation arrangement.

## Recommended close
Keep the first cold message low friction:
- keyword reply OR 15-minute Google Meet
- pilot first
- one clear partner benefit
- bonus only where the partner is eligible and the program has approved written terms
