# v1.0 — Automated Responses + Non-Converter Recycling

## Operating model
Ana and Myrna become relationship closers and exception handlers rather than inbox operators.

### Auto-response eligible
- pricing and published offer questions
- hours/location
- availability/navigation
- tell-me-more
- booking or meeting requests
- partnership interest
- common non-clinical objections
- routine follow-up

### Mandatory human escalation
- clinical/medical questions
- contraindications/adverse events
- legal/refund/complaint
- contracts/custom pricing/negotiation
- explicit request for a person
- ambiguous intent
- high-value opportunities when relationship handling matters
- after two automated replies without resolution

Clinical questions are never answered by the sales automation.

## Non-converter recycle
- engaged/no booking: recycle after 7 days
- meeting/no booking: recycle after 14 days
- no reply after active sequence: long nurture after 30 days
- former clients: reactivation pool after 30 days
- opt-out, hard bounce, bad contact, not interested: suppression; never recycle automatically

Re-entry should use a new relevant reason to contact, not repeat the same message.

## Closed loop
Inbound reply -> classify intent -> auto-response OR owner escalation -> booking/meeting -> Won
                                                        -> no conversion -> recycle pool
Recycle pool -> new relevant campaign/offer -> owner route -> response automation

## Guardrails
- preserve suppression and consent state
- no PHI in marketing automation
- no diagnosis/treatment advice
- no invented availability or pricing
- only published/approved offers may be auto-quoted
- automated messages identify Rescu clearly and provide a human path
- store response classification and outcome for audit/revenue learning
