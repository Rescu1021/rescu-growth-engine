## Audience and positioning

Patients researching ozone therapy, including those with questions about Lyme disease, long COVID, POTS or herpes, should encounter balanced, physician-led information. This content must **not** suggest that ozone cures, treats, prevents, replaces established care for, or is FDA-approved for any of those conditions. Do not use disease labels next to smiling recovered patients, before/after results or claims of antimicrobial clearance. Do not represent experimental molecular mechanisms as established clinical benefit.

**Hook / 20s short:** "Ozone therapy: what does the evidence say?"

**Evidence line:** "A 2026 review found ozone therapy evidence inconsistent and often low certainty."

**CTA:** "Discuss the evidence with RESCU" → https://rescu.life/book (a request, not confirmed appointment).

20s short and draft scene-level VTT are automatically created by the existing Content Studio; timestamps need real-voice synchronization before release.

## Research/source register — must be opened and checked by clinical editor

1. [2026 umbrella review of RCT meta-analyses](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13303763/): conclusions describe inconsistent findings with generally low/very-low-certainty evidence, not justification for routine use.
2. [21 CFR §801.415](https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/21/801.415): federal ozone-device regulatory text; specifically addresses toxic effects and unsupported medical claims.
3. [Medical ozone mechanisms and evidence review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13253314/): mechanistic plausibility does not establish disease-specific clinical effectiveness.
4. [RESCU services](https://rescu.life/treatments) and [booking request](https://rescu.life/book): verify the current ozone consultation entry and the exact live CTA destination.

**Important:** Mechanistic hypotheses, observations from Europe/Spain/Russia, and small uncontrolled studies are not proof of benefits for Lyme, post-COVID syndrome, POTS or herpes. No geographic/regulatory approval equivalence claims without explicit verification.
