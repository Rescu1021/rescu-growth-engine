- 60-second vertical (9:16) video, H.264 MP4 at 720 × 1280, AAC audio.
- Fast kinetic captions / lyric-video pacing, 12 moving five-second shots.
- Original synthesized instrumental beat, no sampled copyrighted music.
- Official RESCU logo (sourced from the RESCU Google Drive) and, in the produced reel, a RESCU HBOT-in-office photograph.
